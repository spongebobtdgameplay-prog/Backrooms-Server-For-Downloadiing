V0.3.21 rebuilds the Windows gameplay HUD clarity and sprint display. Gameplay HUD text no longer uses the two offset shadow passes that created a ghosted transparent-looking duplicate behind labels. Objective, FPS, build text, sprint text, and interaction prompts now render as clean opaque text. The sprint label and bar are aligned from the text raster metrics instead of separate guessed Y positions, the bar is thicker and easier to read, and stamina color smoothly changes from red at low stamina through orange at medium stamina to green at high stamina.
