V0.3.34 replaces the static clown-like entity presentation with a dark animated humanoid silhouette that visibly walks, leans, bobs, and swings its limbs while chasing. The repeating laugh cue was removed, entity pursuit is faster and repaths more often, release grace is shorter, and the gameplay minimap now uses thinner route lines plus smaller, simpler breaker, exit, threat, waypoint, and player markers. Edge clipping remains intact.
