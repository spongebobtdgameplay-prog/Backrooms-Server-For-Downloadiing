V0.3.19 removes the black gameplay HUD slabs and returns the in-game interface to a transparent overlay over Level 0. The sprint label and stamina bar are now brighter, slightly larger, and shadowed for readability without adding a background box. Main-menu and pause buttons now follow the browser version's real button CSS: no side borders, no permanent filled rectangle, top and bottom divider lines only, 4px resting side padding that animates to 14px on hover, and the same subtle hover fill. The FPS display now samples the real SDL performance counter every 0.15 seconds instead of deriving FPS from clamped game time, and shows one decimal so drops and small frame-time changes are visible instead of appearing stuck at 60.
