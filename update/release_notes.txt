V0.3.32 fixes the gameplay minimap and wall-mounted breakers. The gameplay minimap now rotates around the player so the current camera direction always stays at the top while the full map remains world-fixed. Minimap breaker, exit, entity, waypoint, and player markers are reduced so they no longer cover the maze. Breakers are mounted farther out from the wall to account for the open cabinet model, use collision dimensions that match the visible model more closely, accept a slightly wider aiming target and longer interaction distance, and show a visible red indicator before activation and green indicator after activation.
