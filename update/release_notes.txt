V0.3.33 cleans up the gameplay minimap presentation. Gameplay minimap markers no longer disappear when their center leaves the viewport; they are drawn normally and clipped by the minimap bounds so partially visible icons remain at the edge. Breaker, exit, entity, and player markers were redesigned into smaller, simpler shapes, and local maze walls use a thinner one-pixel stroke to reduce the chunky appearance while the camera-relative rotation remains unchanged.
